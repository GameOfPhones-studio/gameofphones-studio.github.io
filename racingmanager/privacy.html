Privacy Policy

Game Of Phones · Racing Manager (working title) · Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what data our racing manager game for Android uses, and why. The short version: we don't collect any personal data ourselves. The game runs offline on your phone. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google's ad service (AdMob), so the game can show rewarded ads.

Who we are

The game is made by Game Of Phones, an independent game studio in Aalborg, Denmark. Contact: gameofphones.dev@gmail.com.

Data the game keeps on your phone

  • Your careers and save games, and small settings such as which tips you've already seen, are stored only on your device.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We have no server, and we never receive your saves.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows optional rewarded ads. When you choose to watch one, you get a reward in the game. The ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example ads shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message when it starts, before personalised ads are used. You can accept or decline. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with PRIVACY SETTINGS on the game's start screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. Because we don't hold any personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best made to Google (see the links above). You can still contact us at gameofphones.dev@gmail.com with any question. You also have the right to complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If the game starts using data in a new way, for example new ad partners or online features, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
